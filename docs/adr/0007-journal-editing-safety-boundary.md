# Separate ordinary Journal editing from advanced progression overrides

The Journal presents safe knowledge edits and deliberately risky progression repair through different modes. Monster knowledge and Formula grants are editable normally: Monster acquisition follows the observed book-style `s/1` Journal entry, while granting a potion, oil, or bomb Formula updates every corresponding Journal and Player alchemy collection without pretending that a source book was read. Oils and bombs follow the potion model but remain marked unverified until checked in-game. Formula removal and all other knowledge categories require session-scoped Advanced Journal Editing.

Advanced Journal Editing also permits a raw root Quest-phase override to an existing phase in that Quest. This operation intentionally does not replay scripts or synchronize NPC, dialogue, reward, story, or linked-quest state; the UI must warn about that limitation and show the exact pending change. It uses the normal Apply/Save/Save As flow and automatic backup rather than forcing Save As. Quests remain readable when advanced editing is off, and unfamiliar Mod categories remain visible without being misclassified.
